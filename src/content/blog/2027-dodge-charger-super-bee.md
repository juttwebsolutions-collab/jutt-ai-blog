# 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee: The 600-HP Muscle Car Is Back

The **2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee** is officially back, and Dodge has given the legendary nameplate a serious modern performance upgrade.

Instead of simply bringing back the badge, Dodge has turned the new Super Bee into the most powerful gasoline-powered version of the current-generation Charger yet. The big headline is a **600-horsepower twin-turbo 3.0-liter Hurricane inline-six**, paired with all-wheel drive and a performance-focused setup. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

For Dodge fans who wanted something more aggressive than the regular Charger SIXPACK models, the new Super Bee could be one of the most interesting Charger variants of this generation.

## 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee Overview

The Super Bee name has a long history with Dodge. The original Super Bee appeared on the Dodge Coronet in 1968 before the name reached the Charger in 1971.

The badge later returned to the modern Charger several times, including special editions during the previous generation.

Now, for 2027, Dodge is bringing the Super Bee name back to the latest Charger platform with a completely different powertrain philosophy.

The new model uses the **High Output version of Dodge's 3.0-liter Hurricane inline-six**, rather than a HEMI V8. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

That means the 2027 Super Bee is designed to combine the traditional Dodge muscle-car attitude with modern turbocharged performance.

## 600 Horsepower From the Hurricane Inline-Six

The biggest story is what's under the hood.

The 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee produces:

- **600 horsepower**
- **531 lb-ft of torque**
- **3.0-liter twin-turbocharged inline-six**
- **All-wheel drive**
- **8-speed TorqueFlite 880RE automatic transmission**

Dodge achieved the additional output through a number of hardware and calibration changes.

The Super Bee uses larger **58-mm Garrett turbochargers**, which can flow approximately 7.5 percent more air than the outgoing turbochargers. Dodge also revised the intake system, charge-air path and intercooling system to support the higher output. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

This gives the Super Bee a substantial jump over the 550-hp Charger Scat Pack's standard High Output SIXPACK configuration.

## 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee Performance

The numbers are where the new Super Bee becomes especially interesting.

Dodge says the 600-hp Super Bee can accelerate from:

**0–60 mph in approximately 3.6 seconds.**

It can also complete the quarter mile in approximately:

**11.8 seconds.** :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Those numbers put the new Super Bee firmly into serious performance-car territory.

The combination of a high-output turbocharged engine and all-wheel drive should also give the Charger strong traction when launching from a standstill.

For buyers who genuinely need six seats, the Model Y L could be one of the most interesting premium electric SUVs available in its market.

For everyone else, the standard Model Y may provide enough space and performance without the additional cost and size.

**Overall, the Tesla Model Y L is best understood as a family-focused evolution of the Model Y rather than simply a stretched version of the same SUV.**
