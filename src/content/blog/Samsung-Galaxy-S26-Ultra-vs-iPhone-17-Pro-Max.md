## Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra vs iPhone 17 Pro Max: Which One Should You Buy?

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** if your priorities are:

- Higher resolution display with more pixels per inch
- More powerful Android performance with 16GB RAM
- 200MP main camera with f/1.4 wider aperture
- 8K video recording capability
- Built-in S Pen stylus for productivity
- World-first Privacy Display technology
- Faster 60W wired charging with charger included
- DeX desktop mode for PC-like productivity
- 7 years of software updates
- Saving Rs.1,40,000

Choose **iPhone 17 Pro Max** if your priorities are:

- Apple iOS ecosystem — iMessage, AirDrop, FaceTime, MagSafe
- ProRes video recording for professional content creation
- 4K@120fps slow motion video
- Best-in-class 3D Face ID security
- MagSafe 25W wireless charging
- Apple Intelligence features
- Highest resale value in Pakistan
- Long-term iOS software experience

### Final Choice

On pure specifications, the **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** wins this comparison convincingly — better display, more RAM, bigger camera system, faster charging, charger included, S Pen included, and Rs.1,40,000 less expensive. The **iPhone 17 Pro Max** wins on ecosystem lock-in, ProRes video, and resale value. If you are not committed to iOS, the Galaxy S26 Ultra is the smarter purchase in 2026.
