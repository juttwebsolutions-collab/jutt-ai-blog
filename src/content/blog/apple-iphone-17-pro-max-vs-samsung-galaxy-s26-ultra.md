## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max** if:

- You shoot cinematic videos using 4K 120FPS Dolby Vision, ProRes RAW, and Apple Log 2.
- You prefer the seamless Apple iOS ecosystem (AirDrop, iMessage, Mac integration).
- You want a versatile 48MP ultra-wide lens and high-end front TrueDepth sensor.
- You prioritize the highest resale market value retention in Pakistan.

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** if:

- You want the new hardware Privacy Display to protect your screen from peeping eyes in public.
- You need the built-in S Pen stylus for precise note-taking and sketching.
- You want a versatile quad camera setup with dedicated 3x and 5x optical telephoto lenses.
- You want faster 60W wired charging speeds to refill quicker.
- You want Samsung DeX desktop mode to turn your phone into a PC workstation.
- You want 7 full years of Android updates while saving Rs. 40,000.
