# CSCO Stock Today: Cisco Earnings Beat Estimates as AI Networking Demand Surges

**Cisco Systems (NASDAQ: CSCO)** is in the spotlight today after the networking giant delivered stronger-than-expected fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 results.

The company reported record quarterly revenue while demand for networking equipment used in artificial intelligence data centers continued to accelerate.

Yet despite the strong numbers, **CSCO stock came under pressure after the earnings release**.

That reaction tells investors something important: Cisco's problem is no longer simply whether the company can grow.

The market now wants to know how profitable that growth will be.

## Cisco Earnings Beat Wall Street Expectations

Cisco reported fiscal fourth-quarter revenue of approximately **$17.3 billion**, up about 18% from the same period a year earlier.

Adjusted earnings came in at approximately **$1.22 per share**.

Both numbers exceeded Wall Street expectations, which had been around $16.8 billion in revenue and $1.17 in adjusted EPS. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Cisco also described the quarter as a record period for revenue, adjusted operating income and adjusted earnings per share.

That makes the latest earnings report one of the strongest periods in the company's recent transformation.

## Why CSCO Stock Is Really About AI Now

Cisco has traditionally been known for routers, switches and enterprise networking equipment.

The artificial-intelligence boom is changing that story.

AI data centers require enormous amounts of networking capacity because AI servers constantly exchange data with one another.

That creates an opportunity for Cisco to sell high-performance networking infrastructure to hyperscalers and other large technology companies.

Cisco's AI infrastructure orders reached **$9.3 billion during fiscal 2026**, including approximately $4 billion during the fourth quarter alone from hyperscale customers. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

That is a dramatic increase from earlier periods.

The company's AI infrastructure orders had reached only about $2 billion during fiscal 2025, according to Cisco's previous reporting. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Cisco's Fiscal 2027 Outlook

Perhaps the most important part of the latest report is Cisco's fiscal 2027 guidance.

The company expects full-year fiscal 2027 revenue between approximately:

**$72.2 billion and $73.4 billion**

That range is well above the roughly $68.7 billion average Wall Street expectation cited by Reuters. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Cisco also expects approximately **$7.5 billion of AI infrastructure revenue** in fiscal 2027.

This suggests that management expects AI networking demand to remain a major growth driver.

## Why CSCO Stock Fell After Strong Earnings

This is the part that may confuse investors.

If Cisco beat earnings and raised its outlook, why did the stock fall?

The answer appears to be **expectations and margins**.

CSCO stock had already risen sharply during 2026 as investors positioned Cisco as an AI infrastructure beneficiary.

Going into the earnings report, shares had gained more than 60% for the year. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

That meant the market was already expecting strong results.

A good earnings report therefore wasn't necessarily enough.

Investors wanted an even bigger upside surprise.

## Gross Margin Is Becoming a Key Issue

Cisco's revenue growth is accelerating, but margins are facing pressure.

The company reported an adjusted gross margin of approximately **66.3%** for the fourth quarter, compared with 68.4% a year earlier. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

The decline reflects the changing product mix and higher component costs.

Cisco expects first-quarter fiscal 2027 adjusted gross margin of roughly **65% to 66%**, slightly below the market expectation cited by Reuters. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

This is an important issue for investors.

AI infrastructure can produce huge revenue opportunities, but hardware-heavy growth can also carry lower margins than software and services.

## Cisco Is Moving Beyond Traditional Networking

Cisco's transformation isn't limited to AI networking hardware.

The company has been expanding its software, cybersecurity and observability businesses.

Its acquisition of **Splunk** was a major part of that strategy.

The goal is to combine Cisco's networking infrastructure with software and security capabilities.

That could make Cisco less dependent on traditional hardware cycles over time.

## What Investors Should Watch Next

There are several numbers that could determine where CSCO stock goes from here.

### AI Revenue

Cisco expects approximately $7.5 billion of AI infrastructure revenue in fiscal 2027.

Investors will want to see whether that target grows during future earnings reports. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

### Gross Margins

Margin performance could become increasingly important as Cisco expands its AI hardware business.

If revenue continues rising but margins fall significantly, investors may question the quality of the growth.

### Data Center Switching

Cisco's data-center networking business is benefiting directly from AI infrastructure expansion.

Continued order growth would strengthen the long-term AI thesis.

### Software and Security

Cisco's ability to grow recurring software and security revenue could help offset pressure from hardware-heavy AI sales.

### Free Cash Flow

Cisco remains a mature technology company with substantial cash generation.

Investors will want to see whether the AI investment cycle translates into stronger long-term cash generation.

## CSCO Stock Bull Case

The bullish case for Cisco is becoming increasingly connected to AI.

The company now has exposure to a massive infrastructure buildout involving:

- AI data centers
- High-speed networking
- Data-center switching
- Enterprise AI infrastructure
- Cybersecurity
- Network security
- Software
- Observability

The latest earnings report provides evidence that AI demand is already translating into meaningful orders.

If AI networking remains strong while Cisco maintains healthy margins, the company could continue growing faster than it did during the previous networking cycle.

## CSCO Stock Bear Case

The biggest risk is that investors have already priced in much of the AI opportunity.

Cisco shares had already experienced a major rally before the latest earnings report. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

That creates a high valuation hurdle.

Other risks include:

- Falling gross margins
- Higher component costs
- Hardware-heavy product mix
- Slower AI order growth
- High expectations
- Competition in networking
- Integration risks from acquisitions
- Potential slowdown in technology spending

The market may punish Cisco even when the company reports strong results if future growth doesn't accelerate enough.

## Is Cisco Stock a Buy?

The latest earnings report strengthens the fundamental case for Cisco.

Revenue growth is accelerating, AI infrastructure orders are rising and fiscal 2027 revenue guidance came in above Wall Street expectations. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

But investors should also consider valuation and margins.

The key question isn't simply:

**"Is Cisco benefiting from AI?"**

The answer is clearly yes.

The more important question is:

**"Can Cisco turn the AI networking boom into sustainable high-margin growth?"**

That will determine whether the current valuation can be supported over the long term.

## CSCO Stock Outlook

Cisco appears to be entering a new phase.

For years, the company was viewed primarily as a mature networking business.

The AI infrastructure boom has changed that perception.

Cisco's networking equipment is becoming an important part of the infrastructure required to connect increasingly powerful AI systems.

The latest earnings report provides evidence that this demand is real.

However, investors should watch margins just as closely as revenue.

Strong AI-driven revenue growth combined with stable profitability would be the ideal scenario for CSCO shareholders.

## Final Verdict

**CSCO stock has a strong AI growth story, but expectations are now extremely high.**

Cisco delivered better-than-expected fiscal Q4 results, with revenue rising to approximately $17.3 billion and adjusted EPS reaching $1.22. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

The company also expects fiscal 2027 revenue of approximately $72.2 billion to $73.4 billion and sees $7.5 billion in AI infrastructure revenue. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

The biggest issue to watch is margins.

Cisco is proving that AI can drive its networking business higher, but investors now need to see whether that growth can translate into strong and sustainable profits.

For now, **AI networking demand is the biggest reason to watch CSCO stock, while margins and valuation remain the biggest reasons for caution.**
