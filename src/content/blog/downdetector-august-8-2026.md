## What Is Downdetector?

Downdetector is an outage-monitoring platform that tracks reports of problems with online services.

Users can visit the website, select a service and see whether reports are currently higher than normal.

The platform can show information such as:

- Current outage reports
- Report volume over time

## How Does Downdetector Work?

Downdetector primarily relies on user-submitted reports and other signals to identify unusual increases in complaints.

When a large number of people report the same service around the same time, the platform can show a spike.

This does **not** necessarily mean that every user of the service is experiencing an outage.

A spike may represent:

- A nationwide outage
- A regional outage
- Problems with a particular feature
- Login problems
- Server problems
- Network issues
- A temporary technical failure

Downdetector therefore works best as an indicator rather than an official confirmation from the affected company.

## Is Downdetector Accurate?

Downdetector can be very useful for identifying unusual spikes in service problems, but its reports should be interpreted carefully.

The number displayed on Downdetector is based on reports and does not necessarily equal the actual number of affected users.

For example, during major outages, news organizations frequently use Downdetector data to demonstrate how quickly user reports are increasing. Reuters has also noted that actual affected users can differ from the number shown by the outage tracker because reports are user-submitted. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## What Does a Downdetector Spike Mean?

A sudden spike generally means that significantly more users are reporting problems than normal.

For example, if a service normally receives very few reports but suddenly receives thousands, that can indicate a developing outage.

However, a spike alone does not tell you exactly what caused the problem.

The issue could be related to:

- Company servers
- Internet infrastructure
- DNS
- Authentication systems
- A mobile carrier
- A regional network
- A specific app feature

## Is the Internet Down or Is It Just You?

One of the biggest advantages of Downdetector is that it helps answer this question.

If only your device is having trouble, possible causes include:

- Wi-Fi problems
- DNS issues
- Browser cache
- App bugs
- Device settings
- ISP problems

If thousands of users are reporting the same problem at the same time, the issue is more likely to be on the service's side.

## What to Do During an Outage

If Downdetector shows a large spike, there may be nothing wrong with your device.

You can:

1. Check the service's official status page.
2. Wait a few minutes and try again.
3. Restart the affected app.
4. Check your internet connection.
5. Try another network.
6. Avoid repeatedly changing account settings.
7. Wait for the company to resolve the incident.

If the outage is widespread, reinstalling the app usually will not fix a server-side problem.
