## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Google Pixel 11 Pro XL** if:

- You prefer clean, bloatware-free stock Android 16 with direct Google feature updates.
- You want an ultra-bright 3600 nits display panel for bright outdoor visibility.
- You want 16GB of RAM standard for heavy background multitasking.
- You love Google's AI photo editing tools and Video Boost processing.
- You want to save Rs. 60,000 on your purchase price.

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** if:

- You want the hardware Privacy Display to block side peeping eyes in public places.
- You need the built-in S Pen stylus for professional note-taking and drawing.
- You want a versatile quad camera system with dedicated 3x and 5x optical telephoto lenses.
- You want faster 60W wired charging speeds.
- You want Samsung DeX mode to turn your phone into a full PC desktop environment.
