# GTA 6 Controller Overview

First revealed during PlayStation's September 2026 State of Play showcase, the **Grand Theft Auto VI Limited-Edition DualSense controllers** celebrate the return to Leonida and Vice City. Rather than releasing a single colorway, Sony designed **two distinct versions** representing the day-and-night contrast of Vice City.

Key highlights include:

- **Two Exclusive Colorways:** **White Limited Edition** (Vice City Daybreak) and **Black Limited Edition** (Vice City Nightlife).
- **Tactile Palm Leaf Molding:** Raised palm tree patterns molded directly into the left and right controller grips.
- **Color-Shifting Iridescent Finish:** Shimmering gradient accents blending sunset pink, violet, and cyan hues.
- **Custom Touchpad & Button Accents:** Features the Roman numeral **VI** printed across the touchpad alongside neon pink D-pad and face button highlights.
- **Multi-Platform Compatibility:** Fully compatible with PS5, Windows PC, Mac, and iOS/Android mobile devices.

# Design Details & Custom Vice City Aesthetics

Sony’s hardware design team incorporated several subtle Easter eggs and tactile upgrades into these controllers:

1. **Molded Palm Tree Grips:** Instead of flat stickers, the palm leaf silhouettes on both handles are physically raised in the plastic shell, offering extra grip during long gaming sessions.
2. **Color-Shifting Bottom Trim:** The lower grip section features a metallic, glittery gradient that shifts between Vice City sunset pink, ocean blue, and neon purple depending on room lighting.
3. **Engraved Rear Logo:** Flipping the controller over reveals the official *Grand Theft Auto VI* insignia engraved cleanly on the back shell.

# Hardware Specs & GTA 6 DualSense Features

Underneath the custom Vice City shell, both controllers pack the full suite of PlayStation 5 DualSense technologies tailored for *GTA VI*:

- **Adaptive Triggers:** Dynamic tension resistance simulates vehicle throttle pressure, brake lockups, and distinct weapon trigger pulls for Jason and Lucia.
- **Haptic Feedback:** Dual actuators translate road textures, coastal rainstorms, explosions, and engine vibrations directly into your palms.
- **Built-in Microphone & Speaker:** Integrated mic array with mute button for quick voice chat, plus controller-speaker audio effects.
- **Motion Controls & Touchpad:** 6-axis motion sensing and clickable multi-touch capacitive touchpad.
- **USB-C Charging:** Internal rechargeable battery charged via USB Type-C *(Note: USB-C cable is not included in the box)*.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## When does the GTA 6 PS5 controller release?
Both the Black and White Grand Theft Auto VI Limited-Edition DualSense controllers release on **November 19, 2026**, alongside the launch of *GTA VI*.

## Does the GTA 6 controller come with a copy of the game?
No. The standalone controllers ($84.99 / £74.99) include only the DualSense wireless controller and user manual. However, select retailers like Very in the UK are offering bundle deals that pair the White controller with a physical copy of *GTA VI*.

## Can I use the GTA 6 DualSense controller on PC or mobile?
Yes. Both Limited-Edition DualSense controllers connect via Bluetooth or USB-C to **Windows PCs, Macs, iPhones, iPads, and Android devices**.

## Why is the Black GTA 6 controller harder to find than the White one?
In major markets like the US and UK, Sony made the **Black Limited Edition** exclusive to its official **PlayStation Direct** store, whereas the **White Limited Edition** is distributed across third-party retailers.
