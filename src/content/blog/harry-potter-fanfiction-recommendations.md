# 5 Harry Potter Fanfiction Books Worth Reading

If you love Harry Potter, fanfiction can open a huge new world.

These stories take familiar characters in completely new directions.

Some explore alternative timelines and different versions of Hogwarts.

Others focus on romance, politics, time travel, or darker themes.

The best part is the sheer amount of content available.

Here are five Harry Potter fanfiction stories worth checking out.

## 1. Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality

**Author:** Eliezer Yudkowsky

This is a great starting point for fanfiction newcomers.

The story follows a different version of Harry Potter.

Harry grows up with a highly educated and scientific father.

Because of this, Harry approaches magic very differently.

Instead of simply accepting magical rules, he wants to understand them.

He questions spells, magical systems, and their underlying principles.

Harry basically treats Hogwarts like a giant scientific experiment.

That creates some fascinating situations.

His intelligence also changes his relationships with Hermione and other students.

The story is very different from Rowling's original Harry.

It is also extremely long.

The story contains around 662,000 words.

For comparison, all seven Harry Potter books total around 1.1 million words.

### Why read it?

- Strong focus on logic and science
- A completely different Harry
- Interesting magical experiments
- Complex characters
- Huge amount of content

It also has a fan-made audiobook.

That makes the enormous word count easier to handle.

---

## 2. The Debt of Time

**Author:** Sha Lonnie

The Debt of Time takes a very different approach.

This story focuses heavily on Hermione.

The plot introduces time travel and changes major events.

Sirius Black's fate becomes especially important.

Hermione discovers a possible way to bring Sirius back.

That decision changes the entire timeline.

Harry, Hermione, and Sirius then face events differently.

The story explores relationships and family connections.

It also adds romance without making romance the entire story.

The fanfiction is extremely long.

It contains more than 700,000 words.

That gives the story plenty of room to develop.

### Why read it?

- Strong Hermione storyline
- Time travel
- Sirius Black gets major attention
- Emotional character development
- Romance mixed with adventure

If you enjoy emotional stories, this is a strong choice.

---

## 3. The Riel Black Chronicles

The Riel Black Chronicles takes place in an alternate universe.

Here, Voldemort does not become the Dark Lord.

Instead, Lord Riddle becomes a powerful politician.

His political movement supports pure-blood ideology.

This creates a very different wizarding society.

Hogwarts also becomes restricted to pure-blood students.

There is another major change.

Harry Potter is actually Harriet Potter.

Harriet is extremely talented with potions.

She desperately wants to study at Hogwarts.

However, her blood status prevents that.

A complicated plan eventually allows Harriet to enter Hogwarts.

She takes another person's identity.

This leads her into Slytherin.

She also becomes closely connected with Snape.

The story currently spans several very long books.

Together, they contain roughly 1.4 million words.

### Why read it?

- Alternate universe
- Political storyline
- Harriet Potter
- Slytherin setting
- Strong Snape storyline
- Huge amount of content

The story is also known for its detailed world-building.

---

## 4. Backwards With Purpose

**Author:** deadwoodpecker

Backwards With Purpose focuses on time travel.

The story begins after a devastating Battle of Hogwarts.

Harry, Ron, and Ginny survive.

Most of their friends and family are gone.

The wizarding world has also suffered enormous losses.

The survivors eventually consider another option.

What if they could return to the past?

They discover a dangerous method involving the Tears of Merlin.

The ritual sends them back to Harry's first year.

However, they remember everything from their original timeline.

That creates a fascinating problem.

They know future events before they happen.

They know important secrets about other characters.

They also know what caused the original disaster.

But changing history can create new problems.

The story explores those consequences carefully.

### Why read it?

- Time travel
- Alternate timeline
- Mystery elements
- Future knowledge
- Strong character interactions

It is also shorter than some recommendations here.

That makes it a good choice after longer stories.

---

## 5. Manacled

**Author:** SenLinYu

Manacled is one of the darkest recommendations here.

Its story takes place after Voldemort wins.

Harry is dead.

The wizarding world is controlled by Voldemort.

The remaining characters face a brutal new reality.

Hermione becomes central to the story.

The story explores oppression, survival, and difficult choices.

It contains extremely dark themes.

These include violence, torture, and forced relationships.

Because of that, this story is not for everyone.

It is much darker than the original Harry Potter books.

Despite those themes, the story became extremely popular.

It was later adapted into a published novel called **Alchemised**.

### Why read it?

- Dark alternate universe
- Strong Hermione storyline
- Complex character relationships
- High emotional stakes
- Detailed world-building

**Content warning:** This story contains very disturbing themes.

Check the warnings before reading it.

## Which Harry Potter Fanfiction Should You Read First?

If you are completely new to fanfiction, start with **Methods of Rationality**.

It provides a very different Harry Potter experience.

If you enjoy emotional stories, try **The Debt of Time**.

If you like politics and alternate universes, try **The Riel Black Chronicles**.

For time travel, **Backwards With Purpose** is a strong option.

For darker stories, **Manacled** is the obvious choice.

## Final Thoughts

Harry Potter fanfiction is much bigger than many people realize.

There are thousands of stories exploring different possibilities.

Some completely change Harry's personality.

Others change the entire wizarding world.

Time travel and alternate universes are especially popular.

The five stories above offer very different experiences.

If you want a long reading journey, start with **Methods of Rationality**.

Then explore the other stories based on your favorite themes.

You may discover an entirely new side of the Harry Potter universe.
