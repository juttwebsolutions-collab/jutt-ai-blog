## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Honor 200 5G** if:

- You want a dedicated 50MP 2.5x optical telephoto zoom lens for natural DSLR-like background blur.
- You want Studio Harcourt Paris black-and-white and color portrait lighting modes.
- You need a massive 512GB of internal storage space out of the box.
- You want blazing fast 100W charging speed (full battery in 35 minutes).
- You want an IR Blaster to control home TV and AC appliances.
- You want maximum camera and storage value while saving Rs. 10,000.

Choose **Vivo V40 5G** if:

- You require full IP68/IP69 water submersion protection rating.
- You want a larger 5500mAh battery cell for multi-day usage.
- You prefer ZEISS optics color rendering and Smart Aura Light ring flash.
- You want a higher-resolution 50MP ultra-wide camera lens.
- You want an ultra-bright 4500 nits screen for direct outdoor sunlight.
