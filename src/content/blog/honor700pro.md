# Honor 700 Pro Overview

The Honor 700 series represents a major design refresh for the brand. Moving away from standard camera bumps, Honor has integrated a functional **1.72-inch secondary display** alongside a high-resolution camera array on the rear panel. 

Alongside this visual overhaul, the Honor 700 Pro targets ultra-heavy users with an unheard-of **9,000 mAh silicon-carbon battery** packaged inside a slim glass-and-aluminum build rated at IP68/IP69K water resistance.

Key expected highlights include:

- **1.72-inch Rear Secondary Screen** for notifications, widgets, and live camera viewfinder.
- **200 MP Primary Camera** with OIS + 50 MP Periscope Telephoto Lens (3.5x optical zoom).
- **Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5** processor with up to 16GB RAM.
- **9,000 mAh Battery** with 80W wired and 50W wireless fast charging.
- **6.57-inch 1.5K AMOLED Display** boasting up to 8,000 nits peak brightness.

# Standout Feature: Secondary Rear Screen

The defining visual element of the Honor 700 Pro is the **1.72-inch AMOLED rear display** embedded directly into the camera array. 

Rather than serving purely as a novelty, early leaks confirm several software-driven features for this mini display:

1. **Live Camera Viewfinder:** Allows users to take high-resolution selfies using the primary 200 MP sensor instead of relying on the front camera.
2. **Glance Notifications:** Check incoming messages, incoming call alerts, and app badges without turning over the main screen.
3. **Quick Controls:** Access music playback controls, active timers, and weather widgets.
4. **Custom Animations:** Personalize the rear aesthetics with custom clocks, dynamic pet avatars, or animated wallpapers.

# Camera System & Imaging Specs

Honor continues its push into mobile photography with a versatile triple rear camera setup on the Pro variant:

- **200 MP Main Sensor:** Large 1/1.4" sensor paired with optical image stabilization (OIS) for low-light clarity and ultra-detailed photography.
- **50 MP Periscope Telephoto:** Equipped with 3.5x optical zoom and hardware OIS to deliver sharp distant shots.
- **12 MP Ultrawide Lens:** Offers a 112-degree field of view along with macro autofocus capabilities.

Combined with the secondary rear display, vloggers and content creators can utilize the 200 MP main camera for 4K video recordings while viewing real-time framing on the back of the phone.

# Battery Life & Fast Charging Capabilities

Smartphone battery capacities have expanded rapidly in 2026, and the Honor 700 Pro leads the charge with an impressive **9,000 mAh silicon-carbon cell**. 

Silicon-carbon technology allows for significantly higher energy density without adding excessive thickness or weight to the body. Paired with power-efficient Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 architecture, the Honor 700 Pro is built to deliver up to **3 days of light-to-moderate use** on a single charge.

When it comes to topping up, the device supports **80W wired charging**, **50W wireless charging**, and **27W reverse wired charging** to charge secondary gear on the go.
