# What is Humble Bundle?

Founded in 2010 and now operating under IGN Entertainment, **Humble Bundle** is a digital storefront famous for its **Pay-What-You-Want (PWYW)** tiered bundles. 

Unlike standard digital stores, Humble curates limited-time collections of Steam games, DRM-free ebooks, and creative software where buyers choose their price tier—and decide how much of their payment goes directly to **charity**, publishers, and Humble.

Key platform highlights include:

- **Tiered Savings:** Unlock progressively larger collections starting as low as $1 to $5, up to full-tier bundles worth over $1,000.
- **Official Steam Keys:** Game bundles provide legitimate, region-verified keys for Steam and other official PC launchers.
- **Built-In Philanthropy:** Every purchase supports verified global charities (such as Save the Children and Freedom to Read Foundation).
- **Humble Choice Membership:** A monthly subscription delivering curated PC games you keep forever.

# How Humble Bundle Pricing & Charity Splits Work

When purchasing any standard bundle on Humble, you have direct control over where your money goes:

1. **Choose Your Tier:** Bundles usually have 3 to 4 price tiers (e.g., $1, $10, $18, $25). Paying for a higher tier unlocks everything in the lower tiers automatically.
2. **Adjust the Sliders:** Click **"Adjust Donation"** before checkout to customize the percentage split between **Charity**, **Publishers/Developers**, and **Humble Tip**.
3. **Redeem Immediately:** Steam keys and DRM-free downloads appear instantly in your Humble account library, though some promotional keys carry expiration dates (always check key redemption deadlines on the bundle page).

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you get to keep Humble Choice games forever?
Yes. Once you claim your monthly Humble Choice games, the Steam keys are yours permanently—even if you cancel or pause your subscription later.

## Are Humble Bundle Steam keys global?
Most keys are global, though certain publishers apply regional restrictions based on your IP address at the time of purchase. Humble always displays regional lock warnings before checkout.

## How often do new bundles launch on Humble Bundle?
New game, book, and software bundles launch multiple times every week (typically on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Fridays), while **Humble Choice** refreshes on the **first Tuesday of every month**.

## Can I pause my Humble Choice membership?
Yes. If a particular month’s games don’t appeal to you, you can pause your subscription for a month with one click and avoid being billed.
