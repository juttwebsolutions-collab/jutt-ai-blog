## iPhone 17 Pro Max vs Infinix Hot 60i: Which One Should You Buy?

Choose **iPhone 17 Pro Max** if your priorities are:

- Best possible camera quality with optical zoom
- Premium OLED display with ProMotion
- Maximum performance for gaming and video editing
- Long-term iOS software support (6+ years)
- Apple ecosystem — AirDrop, iMessage, MagSafe
- 5G connectivity
- High resale value

Choose **Infinix Hot 60i** if you prioritize:

- Best battery life for the price
- 45W fast charging with charger included in the box
- 3.5mm headphone jack
- MicroSD expandable storage
- Infrared blaster
- AI features on a budget
- Spending Rs.5,19,000 less

### Final Choice

The **iPhone 17 Pro Max** wins the specification battle convincingly — better display, better chip, better cameras, better connectivity. But the **Infinix Hot 60i** wins the value battle just as convincingly — bigger battery, faster charging, charger included, and an asking price of Rs.45,999 versus Rs.5,64,999.

Your choice comes down to one question: does the Rs.5,19,000 difference justify what the iPhone gives you over the Infinix? For most buyers in Pakistan, the honest answer is no. For professionals and content creators who need the best tools available, the answer is yes.
