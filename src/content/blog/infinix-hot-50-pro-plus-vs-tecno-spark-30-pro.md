## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus** if:

- You want an elegant 3D curved 120Hz AMOLED display in a budget phone.
- You want the world's slimmest (6.8mm) and lightest (162g) smartphone body.
- You prefer dual stereo speakers tuned by JBL sound engineering.
- You want Gorilla Glass display protection for drop durability.

Choose **Tecno Spark 30 Pro** if:

- You want a higher-resolution 1080p 108MP main camera for detailed photos.
- You need a brighter display screen (1700 nits peak) for direct sunlight.
- You prefer a flat AMOLED screen without curved side reflections.
- You want an integrated IR Blaster to control home TV and AC appliances.
- You want to save Rs. 7,300 while getting the same Helio G100 performance.
