## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Infinix Note 40 Pro** if:

- You want blazing fast 70W wired charging (0-50% in 20 minutes) and 20W wireless MagCharge.
- You want Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) on the 108MP camera for sharp, blur-free photos and videos.
- You prefer stereo speakers tuned by JBL sound engineering.

Choose **Tecno Spark 20 Pro Plus** if:

- You want a lighter, ultra-slim 7.6mm body thickness.
- You want a 32MP selfie camera equipped with dual front LED flashlights.
- You want to save Rs. 15,000 while getting the exact same curved 120Hz AMOLED display and Helio G99 Ultimate performance.
