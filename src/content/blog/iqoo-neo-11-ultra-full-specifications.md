# iQOO Neo 11 Ultra Full Specifications

## Network and Connectivity
*   **Network Bands:** 5G, 4G LTE, 3G, 2G
*   **SIM Slot:** Dual SIM (Nano-SIM, dual stand-by)
*   **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi 7 (802.11 be), dual-band, Hotspot
*   **Bluetooth:** Version 6.0 with A2DP and LE
*   **GPS:** Yes, with dual-band GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO, and BDS
*   **NFC:** Yes
*   **USB:** Type-C 2.0, USB On-The-Go (OTG)

## Body and Design
*   **Dimensions:** 164.8 x 76.5 x 8.6 mm
*   **Weight:** 225 grams
*   **Build Material:** Glass front, aluminum metal frame, glass back panel
*   **Water Protection:** IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance ratings
*   **Rear Styling:** Horizontal pill-shaped camera layout with 3D flowing light dynamic texture
*   **Colors:** Wind Chaser (Blue), Shadow Black, Light White

## Display Features
*   **Screen Type:** Flat AMOLED panel (Visionox F2 luminescent material)
*   **Screen Size:** 6.83 inches
*   **Resolution:** 2K QHD (1260 x 2800 pixels), ~450 ppi density
*   **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz
*   **Peak Brightness:** 2,000 nits full-screen peak brightness
*   **Screen Protection:** Anti-reflective coated protective glass

## System Hardware
*   **Main Chipset:** MediaTek Dimensity 9500M (Custom All-Big-Core architecture)
*   **CPU:** Octa-core (3nm process, peak core clocked at 4.21GHz)
*   **Graphics Chip 1 (GPU):** Mali-G1 Ultra MP12
*   **Graphics Chip 2:** Dedicated iQOO Q2 Gaming Engine Chip
*   **Performance Software:** Monster Hyper-Core tuning engine

## Memory and Storage
*   **RAM Capacity:** 8GB, 12GB, or 16GB LPDDR5X options
*   **Storage Capacity:** 256GB or 512GB (UFS 4.0 high-speed flash)
*   **Card Slot:** No external storage card slot expansion

## Camera System

### Rear Cameras
*   **Primary Sensor:** 50 MP, f/1.8 aperture, wide lens with Autofocus
*   **Stability Feature:** Optical Image Stabilization (OIS)
*   **Secondary Sensor:** 8 MP ultra-wide lens, f/2.2 aperture
*   **Flash Type:** Smart dual-LED flash module
*   **Video Recording:** 4K at 30/60fps, 1080p at 30/60fps

### Front Camera
*   **Selfie Sensor:** 16 MP wide lens, f/2.0 aperture
*   **Lighting Feature:** Screen flash tool for low-light shots
*   **Video Recording:** 1080p at 30fps

## Audio and Sound
*   **Loudspeakers:** Yes, dual stereo speakers 
*   **3.5mm Jack:** No analog audio port included
*   **Audio Enhancement:** Hi-Res wireless audio encoding support

## Software and Features
*   **Operating System:** Android 16
*   **User Interface Skin:** OriginOS 6 (China build)
*   **Security Lock:** Under-display 3D Ultrasonic fingerprint scanner
*   **Sensors:** Accelerometer, gyro, proximity sensor, ambient light sensor, compass

## Battery and Power
*   **Battery Type:** Non-removable Silicon-Carbon battery
*   **Total Capacity:** 9,100mAh
*   **Wired Charging:** 100W flash charging

## Retail Box Contents
*   1 x iQOO Neo 11 Ultra smartphone
*   1 x 100W Flash Charger Adapter
*   1 x USB Type-C to Type-C Power Cable
*   1 x Factory Protective TPU Case
*   1 x Factory Applied Screen Protection Film
*   1 x SIM Card Tray Pin tool
*   1 x Quick Setup Manual Booklet
