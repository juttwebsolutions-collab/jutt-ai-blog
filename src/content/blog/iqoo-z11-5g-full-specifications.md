# iQOO Z11 5G Full Specifications

## Network and Connectivity
*   **Network Bands:** 5G, 4G LTE, 3G, 2G
*   **SIM Slot:** Dual SIM (Nano-SIM, dual stand-by)
*   **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi 6 (802.11 ax), dual-band, Hotspot
*   **Bluetooth:** Version 5.3 with A2DP and LE
*   **GPS:** Yes, with GLONASS, GALILEO, and BDS
*   **NFC:** Yes
*   **USB:** Type-C 2.0, USB On-The-Go (OTG)

## Body and Design
*   **Dimensions:** 163.73 x 76.18 x 8.25 mm
*   **Weight:** 213 grams
*   **Build Material:** Glass front, polycarbonate frame, textured back panel
*   **Durability Ratings:** IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance (Military-grade build)
*   **Rear Styling:** Skyline Camera Module with light-shifting dynamic color finish
*   **Colors:** Aurora Green, Celestial Blue

## Display Features
*   **Screen Type:** 3D Curved AMOLED, 1B colors, HDR support
*   **Screen Size:** 6.83 inches
*   **Resolution:** 1.5K (1260 x 2800 pixels), ~450 ppi density
*   **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz
*   **Dimming Control:** 2160Hz high-frequency PWM dimming
*   **Peak Brightness:** 5,000 nits local peak brightness (2,000 nits HBM)
*   **Eye Care:** Eye Protection Mode, Sleep Comfort Mode, Anti-Fatigue brightness tuning

## System Hardware
*   **Main Chipset:** MediaTek Dimensity 7500 Turbo (Customized performance edition)
*   **CPU:** Octa-core (4nm process)
*   **Graphics Chip (GPU):** Mali-G610 MC4
*   **Internal Cooling:** Heavy-duty Vapor Chamber Liquid Cooling system

## Memory and Storage
*   **RAM Capacity:** 8GB or 12GB LPDDR4X options
*   **Storage Capacity:** 256GB (UFS 3.1 high-speed flash)
*   **Card Slot:** No external storage expansion slot

## Camera System

### Rear Cameras
*   **Primary Sensor:** 50 MP, f/1.8 aperture, wide lens with Autofocus
*   **Sensor Type:** Sony IMX882 image sensor
*   **Stability Feature:** Optical Image Stabilization (OIS)
*   **Secondary Sensor:** 8 MP ultra-wide angle lens, f/2.2 aperture
*   **Flash Type:** Smart dual-LED flash module
*   **Video Recording:** 4K at 30fps, 1080p at 30/60fps

### Front Camera
*   **Selfie Sensor:** 32 MP wide lens, f/2.0 aperture
*   **Lighting Feature:** Screen flash tool
*   **Video Recording:** 1080p at 30fps

## Audio and Sound
*   **Loudspeakers:** Yes, dual stereo speaker setup
*   **3.5mm Jack:** No analog audio port included
*   **Audio Quality:** Hi-Res wireless audio encoding support

## Software and AI Features
*   **Operating System:** Android 16
*   **User Interface Skin:** OriginOS 6
*   **Status Window:** Origin Island (Real-time live status updates bar)
*   **AI Integration:** Circle to Search, AI Captions, AI Creation, AI Routing, AI Transcript Assist
*   **Software Lifecycle:** 3 years of major OS upgrades and 5 years of security updates
*   **Sensors:** Under-display optical fingerprint scanner, accelerometer, gyro, proximity sensor, compass

## Battery and Power
*   **Battery Type:** Non-removable Silicon-Carbon structure (BlueVolt Battery)
*   **Total Capacity:** 7,050mAh
*   **Wired Charging:** 90W superfast flash charging

## Retail Box Contents
*   1 x iQOO Z11 5G smartphone
*   1 x 90W Superfast Power Adapter
*   1 x USB Type-C to Type-C Charging Cable
*   1 x Protective TPU Back Case
*   1 x Factory Applied Screen Protection Film
*   1 x SIM Card Ejector Tray Pin tool
*   1 x Quick Start User Guide Booklet
