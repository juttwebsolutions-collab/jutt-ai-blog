## iQOO Z11 vs Redmi Turbo 5: Which One Should You Buy?

Choose **iQOO Z11** if your priorities are:

- Massive battery
- Lower expected price
- 144Hz display
- Higher selfie camera resolution
- Long battery life
- Better value for money

Choose **Redmi Turbo 5** if you prioritize:

- Stronger performance
- More RAM and storage
- Better storage technology
- Ultrawide camera
- Faster 100W charging
- 4K@60fps video
- Wi-Fi 6E
- Infrared

### Final Choice

The **iQOO Z11** focuses heavily on battery life and value, while the **Redmi Turbo 5** offers a more performance-oriented package with stronger storage, camera versatility and charging.

Your final choice should depend on whether **battery/value** or **performance/features** matter more to you.
