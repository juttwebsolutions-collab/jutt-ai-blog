# Original Review: Anthropic's Claude Meetings

On November 10, 2026, Anthropic expanded Claude from a solo chat and coding assistant into a live multiplayer workspace by launching **Claude Meetings**, **Claude Sheets (Beta)**, **Claude Data** (formerly Claude Dashboards), and upgraded **Interactive Connectors**.

Having tested how these pieces fit into real daily workflows, here is an unfiltered look at what actually holds up—and what still feels like an early v1 release.

### How Claude Meetings Actually Works in Practice
Unlike passive transcription bots that email you a summary ten minutes after everyone hangs up, **Claude Meetings joins your video call as an active participant**. It posts an entry notice for transparency, listens to the live conversation, and builds artifacts *while the meeting is still happening*.

If someone says, *"Let's pull Q4 regional numbers and map out a launch timeline,"* Claude immediately spins up a live spreadsheet in **Claude Sheets** or sketches a diagram on a shared **Claude Whiteboard**. By default, Claude responds via written text in the meeting workspace rather than interrupting speakers out loud.

# What Works Well vs. Where Claude Meetings Falls Short

### The Standout Strengths
- **Work Happens *Before* the Call Ends:** Leaving a 30-minute sync with a populated spreadsheet, structured action items, and a clean whiteboard—rather than a wall of raw transcript text—is genuinely useful.
- **Interactive Connectors Feel Native:** Opening full versions of **Adobe, Box, or Notion** right inside the Claude window (even on Free plans) cuts down tab-switching dramatically. You can ask Claude to edit a Notion doc or retouch an image in place.
- **Permission Inheritance:** Connectors respect existing user permissions, and workspace admins can lock down read/write access so an AI agent doesn't accidentally overwrite shared files.

### The Real Limitations & Friction Points
- **Human Fact-Checking Is Still Mandatory:** Because Claude pulls live data from connected apps during fast-moving calls, someone on the team still needs to verify retrieved numbers before sharing them with the whole room.
- **Plan Fragmentation:** While Interactive Connectors work across all plans (including Free), full **Claude Meetings** and shared **Whiteboards** are gated behind **Team and Enterprise** subscriptions. Solo Pro users get Sheets and Data but miss out on the full live meeting room experience.
- **Early Ecosystem Size:** Launch-day interactive connectors cover key heavyweights like Adobe, Box, and Notion, but teams relying heavily on niche vertical CRMs will still rely on standard API connectors.
