# ONDS Stock Today: Why Ondas Shares Are in Focus on August 13, 2026

**Ondas Inc. (NASDAQ: ONDS)** is attracting significant attention from investors today as the company reports its **second-quarter 2026 financial results**.

The timing is important.

Ondas has transformed rapidly over the past year, expanding its operations across autonomous systems, drones, counter-UAS technology, robotics and defense-related markets.

The company previously reported record first-quarter revenue of **$50.1 million**, compared with $4.3 million in Q1 2025, and raised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to at least **$390 million**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Now investors are waiting to see whether the company's second-quarter results support that aggressive growth story.

## Why ONDS Stock Is Trending Today

The biggest immediate catalyst is earnings.

Ondas announced that it would report its second-quarter results on **Thursday, August 13, 2026**, covering the quarter ended June 30. Management scheduled a conference call for 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

That makes today's report particularly important for investors who have been watching ONDS during its rapid expansion.

The market will be looking beyond the headline revenue number.

Investors will want to know whether the company's acquisitions, growing defense business and expanding backlog are translating into actual revenue and improving profitability.

## What Does Ondas Do?

Ondas operates across several technology markets.

Its businesses include autonomous drone systems, counter-drone technology, robotics, critical infrastructure intelligence and private wireless solutions.

The company describes its platform as combining autonomous aerial and ground robotic systems with private wireless data solutions. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

That puts ONDS in several high-growth themes at once:

- Autonomous drones
- Counter-UAS technology
- Defense robotics
- AI-enabled infrastructure intelligence
- Industrial inspection
- Private wireless networks

This combination is one reason ONDS has become a closely watched small-cap defense and robotics stock.

## ONDS Revenue Growth Has Been Extremely Fast

One of the biggest numbers investors are watching is revenue growth.

Ondas generated **$50.1 million in Q1 2026 revenue**, representing a roughly ten-fold increase from the same quarter a year earlier. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

The company also said its pro forma backlog reached approximately **$457 million** at the end of the first quarter after accounting for recent acquisitions. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

That backlog is important because it gives investors visibility into potential future business.

However, backlog is not the same thing as recognized revenue.

The company still needs to execute on those orders, integrate acquisitions and convert demand into actual sales.

## The $390 Million 2026 Revenue Target

Ondas raised its full-year 2026 revenue target to at least **$390 million** following its first-quarter results. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Management said this would represent approximately 670% growth from 2025.

That is an extremely ambitious growth rate.

The second-quarter results therefore matter because investors can use them to judge whether the company is progressing toward that target.

If revenue comes in strongly and management maintains or raises its outlook, investors could view that as evidence that the growth strategy is working.

If the company falls short or reduces its outlook, the stock could face pressure.

## Ondas Is Expanding Through Acquisitions

Acquisitions have become a major part of the Ondas growth strategy.

In June, Ondas announced an agreement to acquire **Cyberhawk**, a company focused on critical infrastructure intelligence, drone-based inspection, visual data management and AI-enabled analytics. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

According to Ondas, Cyberhawk has operations in 40 countries, more than 300 customers and approximately $95 million in backlog.

The transaction is expected to add exposure to utility, energy and industrial infrastructure markets. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

This is significant because it potentially gives Ondas a business outside pure defense applications.

## Defense Demand Remains a Major ONDS Catalyst

Defense technology remains one of the strongest parts of the ONDS story.

Ondas previously announced more than **$40 million in new orders for autonomous defense systems** in June as demand for loitering munition systems increased in Europe and the United States. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

The company has also expanded its counter-UAS capabilities through businesses including Sentrycs and Iron Drone.

These markets are attracting attention as governments increase spending on autonomous systems and counter-drone technologies.

For ONDS shareholders, the key question is whether this demand can develop into sustainable, high-margin revenue.

Ondas is operating in several rapidly growing technology markets at the same time.

The company has:

- Rapidly growing revenue
- A large reported backlog
- Defense and counter-UAS exposure
- Autonomous drone technology
- Robotics operations
- AI-enabled infrastructure intelligence
- Significant cash resources
- An acquisition-driven expansion strategy

## ONDS Stock Bear Case

There are also significant risks.

The company's valuation and stock performance already reflect substantial expectations for future growth.

That creates a high bar for earnings.

A company can report strong revenue growth and still see its stock fall if investors expected an even stronger result.

There are also risks related to:

- Acquisition integration
- Stock dilution
- High operating expenses
- Cash utilization
- Execution risk
- Defense contract timing
- Valuation
- Short-term trading volatility

Small-cap growth stocks can move dramatically around earnings, especially when expectations are high.

## Is ONDS Stock a Buy?

It is too early to make that decision based only on today's earnings headline.

The important question is whether **business growth is keeping pace with the valuation investors are assigning to the company**.

A strong earnings report combined with maintained or improved guidance could strengthen the bullish case.

A disappointing revenue number, weaker guidance or concerns about cash usage could create downside pressure.

For traders, today's earnings event may produce significant volatility.

For long-term investors, the more important issue is whether Ondas can turn its rapidly expanding backlog and acquisitions into sustainable revenue and eventually consistent profitability.
