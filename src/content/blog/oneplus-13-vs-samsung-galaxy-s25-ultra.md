## OnePlus 13 vs Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra: Which One Should You Buy?

Choose **OnePlus 13** if your priorities are:

- Ultra-long battery life with a huge 6000mAh silicon-carbon battery
- Blazing fast 100W wired and 50W wireless charging speeds
- 100W SuperVOOC charger included in the box
- Unbelievable 4500 nits peak brightness for outdoor daylight visibility
- Massive 24GB LPDDR5X RAM options for heavy multitasking
- IP69 rating against high-pressure hot water jets
- Clean, fast, and light OxygenOS 15 experience
- Saving a substantial Rs.1,40,000

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra** if your priorities are:

- Built-in S Pen stylus for note-taking, sketching, and remote control
- Anti-reflective Gorilla Glass Armor coating for glare-free viewing
- 200MP main camera and 5x optical telephoto camera versatility
- Samsung DeX mode for desktop productivity
- 7 full years of Android OS and security updates
- Superior brand status and higher resale value retention in Pakistan

### Final Choice

For raw performance per rupee, battery endurance, fast charging, and display brightness, the **OnePlus 13** wins this comparison handily — giving you ultimate flagship specs while saving Rs.1,40,000. However, if your daily workflow demands the S Pen stylus, anti-glare display technology, Samsung DeX, and long-term 7-year update support, the **Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra** remains the ultimate Android luxury device.
