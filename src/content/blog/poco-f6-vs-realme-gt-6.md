## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Poco F6** if:

- You want maximum Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 gaming performance for the lowest price.
- You prefer a flat display screen over curved glass for gaming and glass protection.
- You want a lighter (179g) and slimmest (7.8mm) phone in the hand.
- You want to save Rs. 15,000 while getting the same gaming speed and UFS 4.0 storage.

Choose **Realme GT 6** if:

- You want an ultra-bright 6000 nits 8T LTPO curved AMOLED display.
- You want a dedicated 50MP 2x optical telephoto lens for natural portrait photos.
- You want a larger 5500mAh battery cell for longer daily usage.
- You want blazing fast 120W SUPERVOOC charging (50% charge in just 10 minutes).
- You want Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and IP65 protection durability.
