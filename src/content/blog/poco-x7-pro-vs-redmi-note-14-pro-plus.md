## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Poco X7 Pro** if:

- You prioritize 120 FPS PUBG Mobile and hardcore gaming performance.
- You prefer flat display screens over curved glass to avoid accidental side touches.
- You want the speed advantage of UFS 4.0 storage and LPDDR5X RAM.
- You want maximum speed while saving Rs. 10,000.

Choose **Redmi Note 14 Pro Plus** if:

- You want an elegant curved 1.5K AMOLED display with 3000 nits peak brightness.
- You want 120W HyperCharging speed (0 to 100% in 19 minutes).
- You care deeply about portrait photography, optical telephoto zoom, and superior selfie cameras.
- You want IP68/IP69 water submersion protection and a glass/leather build.
