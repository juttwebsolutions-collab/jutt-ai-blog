## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Realme GT 7 Pro** if:

- You want multi-day battery life with a huge 6500mAh Silicon-Carbon battery.
- You want 16GB RAM and 512GB storage out of the box.
- You want a 50MP periscope telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom.
- You shoot under water using IP69 high-pressure waterproof rating.
- You want a larger 6.78-inch display with 6000 nits peak brightness.
- You want maximum Snapdragon 8 Elite power while saving Rs. 30,000.

Choose **Xiaomi 15** if:

- You prefer a lightweight, compact 6.36-inch flat display phone.
- You want professional Leica camera optics with triple 50MP lenses.
- You need 50W wireless fast charging capability.
- You want a higher-resolution 32MP front selfie camera.
- You prefer Xiaomi HyperOS 2.0 ecosystem integration.
