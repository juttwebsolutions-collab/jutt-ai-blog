## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Redmi Note 13 Pro** if:

- You want a high-resolution 200MP OIS main camera for crisp, detailed photography.
- You prefer a flat display screen over curved glass.
- You need Corning Gorilla Glass Victus protection against drops and scratches.
- You require a MicroSD card expansion slot.
- You want to save Rs. 10,000 on purchase price.

Choose **Infinix Zero 30** if:

- You shoot video blogs and need a 50MP front selfie camera with 4K @ 60FPS recording.
- You want an immersive 144Hz 3D curved AMOLED display panel.
- You want faster Dimensity 8020 5G performance with 90 FPS gaming support.
- You want 12GB RAM standard and JBL-tuned stereo speakers.
