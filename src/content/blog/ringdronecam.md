# Ring Drone Cam Overview

First teased under the concept name *Ring Always Home Cam*, Amazon has refined the hardware and AI stack to formally introduce the **Ring Drone Cam**. 

Weighing just 165 grams, this lightweight indoor quadcopter stays docked when not in use. When an alarm sensor trips or a scheduled patrol begins, the drone lifts out of its charging base, flies a custom route up to 4–5 feet in the air, streams live footage to the Ring app, and docks itself automatically.

Key official highlights include:

- **AI Pilot Engine:** Vision models and obstacle-avoidance algorithms for dynamic room navigation.
- **2K Square Video Recording:** 1920x1920 resolution with an ultra-wide 148° field of view.
- **Custom Patrol Routes:** Automated flights scheduled via Alexa Home Map.
- **Automatic Incident Response:** Deploys instantly when motion, door/window, or glass-break sensors trip.
- **Physical Privacy Shield:** Camera sensors remain physically blocked inside the charging dock.

# AI Pilot & Indoor Flight Navigation

Operating a flying drone inside a home requires precise navigation to avoid walls, furniture, pets, and hanging lights. The Ring Drone Cam uses Amazon's proprietary **AI Pilot** architecture:

1. **Initial Mapping Run:** During setup, users carry the device through their home to generate an accurate floor plan within the **Alexa Home Map** app.
2. **Preset Patrol Points:** Define specific areas of interest, such as front doors, back patios, kitchen counters, or stove tops.
3. **Reactive Obstacle Avoidance:** Real-time computer vision allows the drone to re-route around unexpected objects, open doors, or furniture shifts.
4. **Indoor Geofencing:** Hardware constraints prevent the drone from flying outdoors, adhering to FAA indoor line-of-sight safety parameters.

# Privacy Safeguards & Docking Mechanism

Addressing privacy concerns, Ring designed the Drone Cam with physical security guarantees:

- **Docked Camera Cover:** While resting in the square docking station, the camera lens is physically obstructed, making recording impossible.
- **Audible Flight Profile:** Enclosed propellers generate an audible sound during flight, ensuring occupants are always aware when the drone is actively recording.
- **Recording LED Indicators:** Bright LED status lights illuminate whenever the camera sensor feeds are active.

---

# Incident Response & Smart Integration

When paired with a Ring Alarm system or Ring Protect plan, the Drone Cam acts as an automated security responder:

- **Sensor-Triggered Dispatch:** If a door contact sensor or motion sensor triggers in a specific zone, the Drone Cam immediately launches and flies directly to the area to send a live 2K video feed to your phone.
- **Night Flight Spotlight:** Dual built-in LED spotlights automatically turn on in dark rooms to capture crisp video at night.
- **Remote Flight Controls:** Users can manually trigger a flight path or check specific rooms via the Ring app while away from home.
