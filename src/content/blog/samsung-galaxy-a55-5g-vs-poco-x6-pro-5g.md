## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Samsung Galaxy A55 5G** if:

- You want an aluminum metal frame with Gorilla Glass Victus+ back build.
- You require IP67 full water submersion protection.
- You need a MicroSD card slot to expand internal storage.
- You want a better 32MP selfie camera and 4K selfie video recording.
- You want 4 full years of Android OS and 5 years of security updates.

Choose **Poco X6 Pro 5G** if:

- You prioritize 90 FPS competitive gaming performance (Dimensity 8300 Ultra).
- You want double the storage (512GB UFS 4.0) and 12GB RAM.
- You want a sharper 1.5K (1220×2712) AMOLED display with 1800 nits brightness.
- You want fast 67W charging (100% in 45 mins) with a charger included.
- You want a lighter 186g phone in the hand.
- You want to save Rs. 8,000 on purchase price.
