## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Samsung Galaxy A55 5G** if:

- You want a premium aluminum metal frame and Gorilla Glass Victus+ build.
- You require true IP67 water submersion protection against accidental drops in water.
- You want 4 full years of major Android OS updates and 5 years of security patches.
- You prefer a higher-resolution 32MP front selfie camera.
- You want a faster 4nm Exynos 1480 processor for smoother day-to-day multitasking.

Choose **Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 Pro** if:

- You want an ultra-clear 200MP main camera for high-detail photography and zooming.
- You want blazing fast 67W charging speeds with the power adapter included in the box.
- You prefer a sharper 1.5K (1220×2712) resolution AMOLED screen.
- You want a lighter, slimmer 187g phone body.
- You want to save a massive Rs. 43,000 on your purchase price.
