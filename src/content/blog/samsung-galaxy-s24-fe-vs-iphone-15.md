## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S24 FE** if:

- You want a smooth 120Hz refresh rate AMOLED screen instead of 60Hz.
- You want a dedicated 3x optical telephoto camera for distant zoom shots.
- You need a larger 4700mAh battery for all-day usage.
- You want 256GB base storage standard instead of 128GB.
- You want 7 full years of Android OS and security upgrades.
- You want Samsung DeX desktop mode and Galaxy AI features while saving Rs. 45,000.

Choose **Apple iPhone 15** if:

- You prefer Apple's iOS ecosystem (AirDrop, iMessage, Apple Watch integration).
- You want the Dynamic Island interface notch for notifications and live activities.
- You shoot high-quality 4K Dolby Vision videos for social media platforms.
- You want a lighter (171g) and more compact 6.1-inch form factor.
- You want 3D Face ID biometric security unlock.
- You prioritize maximum resale value retention in the Pakistani mobile market.
