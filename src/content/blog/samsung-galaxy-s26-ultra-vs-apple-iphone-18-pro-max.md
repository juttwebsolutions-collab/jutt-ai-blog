## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** if:

- You want the hardware Privacy Display feature to block unwanted viewers from seeing your screen in public.
- You require the built-in S Pen stylus for effortless writing and note-taking.
- You want flexible camera zooming with dedicated 3x and 5x optical telephoto sensors.
- You want faster 60W wired charging speeds.
- You want Samsung DeX to turn your phone into a complete desktop workstation.
- You want 7 full years of Android upgrades while saving Rs. 70,000.

Choose **Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max** if:

- You want the unmatched raw performance of Apple's 2nm A20 Pro chipset.
- You want a massive 5567mAh battery for multi-day endurance.
- You shoot high-end cinematic content using 4K 120FPS Dolby Vision and ProRes RAW.
- You prefer the secure iOS ecosystem, FaceTime, and iMessage integration.
- You want maximum resale value retention in the local market.
