# Starlink Mini 2 Overview

The original Starlink Mini gained massive popularity for its small footprint and low power draw, but users still had to carry bulky external power stations or custom DC cables. The **Starlink Mini 2** eliminates this friction.

SpaceX re-engineered the back of the dish casing to hold a contoured, snap-in **71 Wh battery**. Additionally, the internal wireless router gets a major boost to **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)**, offering faster local speeds and lower latency for connected devices.

Key highlights include:

- **71 Wh Integrated Swappable Battery:** Delivers up to 4.5 hours of continuous off-grid operation.
- **Native USB-C Power Delivery:** Direct charging via USB-C ports on both the dish and the battery pack.
- **Wi-Fi 6 (AX3000) Router:** Upgraded wireless standard replacing the older Wi-Fi 5 chip.
- **Slightly Thinner Profile:** Re-architected PCB layout reducing overall body bulk.
- **In-Motion Connectivity:** Built-in hardware optimizations designed for vehicle and RV travel.

# Game-Changer: 71 Wh Swappable Battery

The standout feature of the Starlink Mini 2 is its modular power system. 

Instead of an internal battery that permanently adds weight or degrades over time, SpaceX designed an **integrated slide-out battery cartridge** that clips directly to the rear of the dish.

1. **4.5-Hour Runtime:** On standard web browsing and light streaming, the 71 Wh cell provides several hours of cable-free internet.
2. **Standalone Power Bank:** The 1.08 lb battery features its own independent **USB-C port** and **LED charge indicator**, allowing you to charge your phone or laptop from the battery while detached.
3. **Hot-Swappable Workflow:** Users can buy spare battery packs to swap in the field during extended off-grid trips.

# Wi-Fi 6 & Connectivity Upgrades

While the original Mini utilized an older Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) modem, the Starlink Mini 2 upgrades to **Wi-Fi 6 (AX3000)**. 

This provides three key improvements:
- **Greater Device Capacity:** Connect up to 128 devices simultaneously without dropping speed.
- **Better Throughput & Range:** Wi-Fi 6 delivers higher local transfer speeds and handles wall penetration more effectively.
- **Power Efficiency:** Target Wake Time (TWT) features in Wi-Fi 6 help reduce battery consumption on connected smartphones and tablets.
