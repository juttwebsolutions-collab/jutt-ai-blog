# Tecno Pova 8 Pro 5G Full Specifications

## Network and Connectivity
*   **Network Bands:** 5G, 4G LTE, 3G, 2G
*   **SIM Slot:** Dual SIM (Nano-SIM, dual stand-by)
*   **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi 6 (802.11 ax), dual-band, Hotspot
*   **Bluetooth:** Version 5.3 with A2DP and LE
*   **GPS:** Yes, with GLONASS, GALILEO, and BDS
*   **NFC:** Yes
*   **USB:** Type-C 2.0, USB On-The-Go (OTG)
*   **Offline Tools:** Long-range wireless voice and text network (up to 1,500 meters)

## Body and Design
*   **Dimensions:** 164.5 x 76.2 x 7.39 mm
*   **Weight:** 189 grams
*   **Build Material:** Glass front, polycarbonate frame, textured back panel
*   **Rear Lighting:** Alive Matrix Display (177 mini LED beads with 49 custom scenarios)
*   **Colors:** Tundra Green, Graphite Black, Arc White

## Display Features
*   **Screen Type:** HyperLux AMOLED, 1B colors
*   **Screen Size:** 6.78 inches
*   **Resolution:** 2644 x 1208 pixels
*   **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz
*   **Touch Sampling Rate:** 360Hz
*   **Peak Brightness:** 4,500 nits
*   **Eye Care:** 2592Hz high-frequency PWM dimming
*   **Glass Protection:** Corning Gorilla Glass 7i

## System Hardware
*   **Main Chipset:** MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultimate
*   **CPU:** Octa-core (4nm process)
*   **Graphics Chip 1 (GPU):** Mali-G610 MC4
*   **Graphics Chip 2:** Dedicated P1 Graphic Processing Chip
*   **Internal Cooling:** 5,000 mm² Vapor Chamber thermal shield

## Memory and Storage
*   **RAM Capacity:** 8GB or 12GB options
*   **Virtual RAM Extension:** Up to an additional 12GB extended RAM
*   **Storage Capacity:** 256GB or 512GB (UFS 3.1)
*   **Card Slot:** Dedicated MicroSDXC slot

## Camera System

### Rear Cameras
*   **Primary Sensor:** 50 MP, f/1.8 aperture, Sony LYTIA 700C sensor
*   **Focus Features:** Phase Detection Autofocus (PDAF), Optical Image Stabilization (OIS)
*   **Secondary Sensor:** 8 MP ultra-wide lens, f/2.2 aperture
*   **Flash Type:** Quad-LED dual-tone flash
*   **Video Recording:** 4K at 30fps, 1080p at 30/60fps

### Front Camera
*   **Selfie Sensor:** 13 MP wide lens, f/2.0 aperture
*   **Flash Type:** Dual-LED flash embedded in top bezel
*   **Video Recording:** 1080p at 30fps

## Audio and Sound
*   **Loudspeakers:** Yes, dual stereo speakers tuned by DTS
*   **3.5mm Jack:** Yes, legacy audio port included
*   **Audio Quality:** 24-bit/192kHz Hi-Res wireless audio support

## Software and Features
*   **Operating System:** Android 16
*   **User Interface Skin:** HiOS 16
*   **AI Assistant:** Ella Virtual Assistant integration
*   **Sensors:** Side-mounted fingerprint reader, accelerometer, gyro, proximity sensor, compass

## Battery and Power
*   **Battery Type:** Non-removable Silicon-Carbon battery
*   **Total Capacity:** 6,500mAh
*   **Wired Charging:** 45W superfast charging
*   **Battery Health:** Integrated Battery Self-Healing Technology
