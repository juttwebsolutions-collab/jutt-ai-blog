## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Tecno Spark 30 Pro** if:

- You want a sharper 108MP primary camera with 3x lossless zoom capability.
- You want a brighter 1700 nits flat AMOLED screen for easy outdoor visibility.
- You prefer a flat display panel over curved edges.
- You need an integrated IR Blaster remote to control home ACs and TVs.
- You want to save Rs. 7,300 on your purchase price.

Choose **Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus** if:

- You want an upscale 3D curved 120Hz AMOLED display for a flagship look.
- You want the world's slimmest (6.8mm) and lightest (162g) phone body.
- You prefer dual stereo speakers tuned by JBL sound engineering.
- You want gaming bypass charging to prevent overheating during long sessions.
