# Tesla Model Y L: Price, Range, Interior, Specs & Everything You Need to Know

The **Tesla Model Y L** is one of the most interesting additions to Tesla's growing electric SUV lineup.

The extended version of the Model Y brings a longer body, additional passenger space and a six-seat configuration, giving Tesla buyers a more practical option for larger families.

Instead of completely changing the Model Y formula, Tesla has stretched it into a larger and more comfortable package.

The result is an electric SUV that combines Tesla's familiar technology, strong performance and charging ecosystem with significantly more interior flexibility.

## What Is the Tesla Model Y L?

The Tesla Model Y L is essentially a longer and more spacious version of the Model Y.

The biggest difference is the additional length and the three-row seating configuration.

While the regular Model Y is primarily designed around five-seat practicality, the Model Y L is designed to carry **up to six adults**.

The second row uses individual captain's chairs, creating a passage toward the third row.

This makes the Model Y L particularly attractive to families who need additional seating but don't want to move to a much larger full-size electric SUV.

## Tesla Model Y L Design

The Model Y L retains the recognizable shape of the modern Model Y while making the overall vehicle longer.

Tesla has continued its aerodynamic design philosophy instead of giving the Model Y L a boxy traditional SUV appearance.

The extended body provides additional room for passengers while maintaining the smooth roofline that is important for aerodynamic efficiency.

The result is a vehicle that looks familiar but offers a noticeably larger cabin.

## Tesla Model Y L Dimensions

Tesla lists the Model Y L at approximately:

| Specification | Tesla Model Y L |
|---|---:|
| Length | **4,969 mm** |
| Width, mirrors folded | **1,982 mm** |
| Height | **1,668 mm** |
| Ground clearance | **169 mm** |
| Seating capacity | **Up to 6 adults** |
| Maximum cargo capacity | **2,539 litres** |

The longer body is one of the most important differences between the Model Y L and the standard Model Y.

The extra length allows Tesla to create additional passenger space without making the vehicle excessively wide.

That is especially useful for families because a wider SUV can become difficult to maneuver in crowded cities and parking garages.

## Tesla Model Y L Interior

The interior is where the Model Y L becomes much more interesting.

Tesla has focused on making the cabin more suitable for families and multiple passengers.

The vehicle provides three rows of seating, with two individual captain's chairs in the second row.

This layout makes it easier for passengers to access the third row.

The cabin also continues Tesla's minimalist design philosophy.

Instead of filling the dashboard with buttons and traditional gauges, Tesla relies heavily on touchscreen controls and software.

## Six-Seat Configuration

The Tesla Model Y L can seat **up to six adults**.

The seating arrangement is:

- Two front seats
- Two second-row captain's chairs
- Two third-row seats

The captain's-chair configuration gives the second row a more premium feel while also creating an easier path toward the third row.

For families with children, this could make the Model Y L significantly more practical than a five-seat Model Y.

## Second-Row Comfort

The second row receives a number of comfort-focused features.

Tesla lists heated and ventilated second-row seats, powered armrests and one-touch folding functionality.

The second row is therefore designed to feel more like a dedicated passenger area rather than simply an extension of the front cabin.

This becomes particularly useful during longer road trips.

## Third-Row Comfort

The third row is one of the biggest reasons buyers may choose the Model Y L.

Tesla has equipped the third row with features including:

- Heated seats
- Power recline
- Dedicated climate ventilation
- Additional passenger lighting
- Easy access from the second row

The goal is to make the third row usable rather than simply adding two emergency seats.

However, buyers who regularly carry tall adults should still test the third row themselves because comfort can vary depending on passenger height and seating position.

## Tesla Model Y L Cargo Space

A three-row SUV needs to balance passenger space with luggage capacity.

The Model Y L provides up to **2,539 litres of maximum cargo capacity** when the appropriate rear seats are folded.

With all six seats being used, Tesla lists approximately **536 litres of cargo capacity**. ([tesla.com](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/en_jp/GUID-1E76B638-7B12-4D9A-8767-94B7F1E92A0E.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

This gives the Model Y L considerable flexibility.

Owners can use all three rows when passenger capacity is the priority or fold the rear seats when they need additional luggage space.

## Tesla Model Y L Technology

Technology remains one of the strongest parts of the Model Y L.

The cabin includes a large **16-inch front touchscreen** that controls many of the vehicle's functions.

There is also an **8-inch rear touchscreen** for passengers.

This rear display gives second- and third-row passengers access to entertainment and climate-related functions.

Tesla's software-first approach also means the vehicle can receive over-the-air updates.

That allows Tesla to introduce improvements and new features without requiring owners to visit a dealership for every software change.

## Tesla Model Y L Audio System

The premium version of the Model Y L features a **19-speaker Tesla audio system**.

The system is designed to provide an immersive listening experience throughout the cabin.

With passengers spread across three rows, a strong audio system becomes particularly useful for long family journeys.

The rear touchscreen also gives passengers more control over their entertainment experience.

## Tesla Model Y L Performance

Despite being larger than the regular Model Y, the Model Y L is still a performance-oriented electric SUV.

The Premium version uses **dual-motor all-wheel drive**.

Tesla lists a **0–100 km/h time of approximately 5.0 seconds** for the India-market Premium version.

That is impressive for a vehicle designed to carry six adults.

The instant torque of the electric motors also makes acceleration feel smooth and immediate.

## Tesla Model Y L Horsepower and Torque

Tesla does not typically market its vehicles around traditional horsepower and torque figures in the same way as many gasoline manufacturers.

Instead, Tesla focuses heavily on acceleration, range and electric powertrain performance.

The Model Y L's dual-motor configuration provides strong acceleration through the combination of front and rear electric motors.

The all-wheel-drive system also helps improve traction when accelerating from a stop.

## Tesla Model Y L All-Wheel Drive

The Model Y L Premium uses **Dual Motor All-Wheel Drive**.

This means electric motors power both the front and rear axles.

AWD provides several benefits:

- Better traction
- Stronger acceleration
- Improved stability
- Better performance in challenging conditions
- More confidence during highway driving

For a larger six-seat SUV, AWD is especially useful because the vehicle can carry several passengers and substantial luggage.

## Tesla Model Y L Range

Range is one of the most important specifications for any electric SUV.

Tesla lists the Model Y L Premium with a **681 km WLTP range** in India.

That makes the Model Y L particularly interesting for buyers who frequently travel long distances.

However, the WLTP figure should not be considered a guaranteed real-world range.

Actual range can vary depending on:

- Highway speed
- Weather
- Temperature
- Air conditioning
- Passenger load
- Tire pressure
- Driving style
- Road conditions

A driver traveling at high speeds on a hot day may see a significantly different range from the official laboratory figure.

## Tesla Model Y L Charging

The Model Y L supports Tesla's fast-charging ecosystem.

Tesla lists a maximum Supercharging rate of **250 kW**.

The company says the vehicle can add up to **288 km of range in 15 minutes** under its stated testing conditions.

That makes the Model Y L suitable for long-distance travel where charging stops need to remain relatively short.

Actual charging speed can vary depending on battery temperature, state of charge and charger conditions.

## Tesla Model Y L Suspension

The larger Model Y L also receives updated suspension technology.

Tesla uses adaptive damping to help balance comfort and handling.

This is especially important for a three-row SUV because rear passengers can be more sensitive to bumps and road imperfections.

The longer wheelbase can also contribute to a smoother ride by providing greater stability over uneven roads.

## Tesla Model Y L Safety

Safety remains a major part of Tesla's vehicle design.

The Model Y L includes a range of active safety technologies, including:

- Automatic Emergency Braking
- Forward Collision Warning
- Lane Departure Avoidance
- Blind Spot Monitoring
- Multiple cameras
- Advanced driver-assistance features
- Additional airbags for rear passengers

Tesla also emphasizes the structural safety of the vehicle and the protection provided by its battery platform.

## Tesla Model Y L Driver Assistance

Tesla's driver-assistance technology is another major selling point.

Depending on market and configuration, buyers can access Tesla's advanced driver-assistance features.

However, these systems are designed to assist the driver rather than replace the driver.

Tesla states that drivers must remain attentive and responsible while using its driver-assistance systems.

The technology should therefore be viewed as an additional safety and convenience layer rather than fully autonomous driving.

The Model Y L is also longer and offers a larger maximum cargo capacity.

For buyers who don't need three rows, the standard Model Y may remain the better option.

Regenerative braking is another important part of the Model Y L driving experience.
