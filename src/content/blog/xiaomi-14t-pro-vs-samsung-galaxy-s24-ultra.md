## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Xiaomi 14T Pro** if:

- You want top-tier flagship performance while saving a massive Rs. 180,000.
- You want 120W fast charging (100% battery in 19 minutes) with a charger included.
- You prefer a faster 144Hz display panel for ultra-smooth gaming.
- You want Leica Summilux color rendering and portrait photography.
- You want 50W fast wireless charging.

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra** if:

- You want an integrated S Pen stylus for drawing, taking notes, and precise work.
- You want the best camera zoom capabilities with a 200MP sensor and 5x/3x optical lenses.
- You want anti-reflective Gorilla Armor glass for outdoor glare reduction.
- You prefer a Grade 2 Titanium frame build quality.
- You demand 7 full years of major Android OS upgrades.
