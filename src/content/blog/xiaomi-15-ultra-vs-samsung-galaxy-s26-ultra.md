## Which Smartphone Should You Buy?

Choose **Xiaomi 15 Ultra** if:

- You want professional Leica camera optics featuring a massive 1-inch main sensor.
- You want a 200MP Leica periscope telephoto lens for incredible long-range zoom.
- You want faster 90W wired and 80W wireless charging speeds with the charger included in the box.
- You want 16GB RAM and 512GB storage standard while saving Rs. 30,000.

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** if:

- You want the new hardware Privacy Display to block side peeping eyes in public.
- You need the built-in S Pen stylus for precise writing and note-taking.
- You want 7 full years of major Android OS upgrades for long-term security.
- You want Samsung DeX mode to use your smartphone like a desktop PC.
