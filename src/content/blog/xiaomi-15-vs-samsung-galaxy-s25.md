## Xiaomi 15 vs Samsung Galaxy S25: Which One Should You Buy?

Choose **Xiaomi 15** if your priorities are:

- Massive 5400mAh battery life in a compact footprint
- Rapid 90W wired and 50W wireless charging speeds
- 90W fast power adapter included inside the box
- Triple 50MP Leica camera system with 10cm telephoto macro mode
- Sharper 1200p display resolution with 3200 nits peak brightness
- Built-in IR Blaster for controlling TV and AC home appliances
- Saving Rs.46,000 while getting better battery and camera specs

Choose **Samsung Galaxy S25** if your priorities are:

- Extremely light (162g) and super-slim (7.2mm) one-handed design
- Samsung DeX mode for desktop productivity
- Overclocked Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy processor
- Guaranteed 7 years of Android OS and security updates
- High brand recognition and maximum resale value in Pakistan

### Final Choice

For raw battery life, charging speed, camera hardware, and value for money, the **Xiaomi 15** takes a clear win — offering a 5400mAh battery, 90W charging with charger included, and triple 50MP Leica cameras for Rs.219,999. However, if your preference is an ultra-lightweight phone with 7 years of software update support and Samsung DeX, the **Samsung Galaxy S25** remains a top compact choice.
